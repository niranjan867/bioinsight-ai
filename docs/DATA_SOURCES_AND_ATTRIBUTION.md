# Scientific Data Sources, Attribution & Licensing Compliance

This document provides a comprehensive audit of all external scientific data sources, biological databases, APIs, and computational references integrated into **BioInsight AI 2.0**. It outlines the data retrieved, how BioInsight AI utilizes each source, applicable licenses, attribution requirements, rate limits, and scientific disclaimers in accordance with open-science and hackathon compliance standards (EthnoHack 2026).

### 1. NCBI Entrez (National Center for Biotechnology Information)

- **Official Website**: [https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/)
- **API Documentation**: [https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK25500/](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK25500/) (Entrez Programming Utilities / E-utilities)
- **Primary Endpoint Used**: `https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/esearch.fcgi` and `esummary.fcgi`
- **Data Retrieved**:
  - Official gene symbols, names, and aliases
  - Entrez Gene IDs and organism taxonomy (e.g., *Homo sapiens*, TaxID 9606)
  - Chromosomal locations and curated genomic summaries
  - Remote NCBI QBlast sequence similarity searches (`blastn`, `blastp`)
- **How BioInsight AI Uses the Data**:
  - Validates gene identities in the Gene Intelligence module (`/genes`).
  - Cross-references identifiers in the Federated Database Hub (`/databases`).
  - Submits remote sequence alignments via NCBI QBlast in the Sequence Laboratory (`/sequences`).
- **Attribution & Citation Requirement**:
  - NCBI requests that researchers cite the NCBI Resource Coordinators publication:
    > *Sayers EW, et al. Database resources of the National Center for Biotechnology Information. Nucleic Acids Res. 2024 Jan 5;52(D1):D33-D43. doi: 10.1093/nar/gkad1044.*
- **License & Terms of Use**:
  - **Public Domain**: Data generated by NCBI/NLM is in the public domain under 17 U.S.C. § 105.
  - NCBI Website and Data Usage Policy: [https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/)
- **Rate Limits & API Policies**:
  - Limit: Maximum 3 requests/second without an API key; up to 10 requests/second with a personal `NCBI_API_KEY`.
  - Policy: Automated clients must provide `tool` (`NCBI_TOOL=BioInsightAI`) and contact email (`NCBI_EMAIL=...`) parameters. BioInsight AI fully enforces this parameter configuration in `backend/app/core/config.py`.

### 2. UniProtKB (Universal Protein Resource)

- **Official Website**: [https://www.uniprot.org/](https://www.uniprot.org/)
- **API Documentation**: [https://rest.uniprot.org/](https://rest.uniprot.org/)
- **Primary Endpoint Used**: `https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/search` and `https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/{accession}.json`
- **Data Retrieved**:
  - Protein accessions and entry names (Swiss-Prot reviewed & TrEMBL unreviewed)
  - Primary amino acid sequences and sequence lengths
  - Functional descriptions, catalytic activity, and Gene Ontology (GO) terms
  - Subcellular localization annotations
  - Cross-references to experimental structures (RCSB PDB) and predicted models (AlphaFold DB)
- **How BioInsight AI Uses the Data**:
  - Serves as the primary source of truth for the Protein Intelligence module (`/proteins`).
  - Provides amino acid sequences for local physicochemical property calculations via Biopython (molecular weight, isoelectric point, GRAVY, instability index).
  - Acts as an accession resolver for AlphaFold DB queries.
- **Attribution & Citation Requirement**:
  - Mandatory attribution under CC BY 4.0:
    > *The UniProt Consortium. UniProt: the Universal Protein Knowledgebase in 2023. Nucleic Acids Res. 2023 Jan 6;51(D1):D523-D531. doi: 10.1093/nar/gkac1052.*
- **License & Terms of Use**:
  - **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**.
  - UniProt Copyright & License: [https://www.uniprot.org/help/license](https://www.uniprot.org/help/license)
- **Rate Limits & API Policies**:
  - Fair use policy. Clients must avoid excessive concurrent connections and respect HTTP 429 backoff headers.

### 3. RCSB Protein Data Bank (RCSB PDB)

- **Official Website**: [https://www.rcsb.org/](https://www.rcsb.org/)
- **API Documentation**: [https://search.rcsb.org/](https://search.rcsb.org/) and [https://data.rcsb.org/](https://data.rcsb.org/)
- **Primary Endpoint Used**: `https://search.rcsb.org/rcsbsearch/v2/query` and `https://files.rcsb.org/download/{pdb_id}.pdb`
- **Data Retrieved**:
  - 3D macromolecular structures solved via X-ray crystallography, cryo-EM, and NMR
  - Crystallographic resolution ($\text{\AA}$), deposition dates, and release dates
  - Macromolecular polymer chains and co-crystallized hetero-residues / ligands (e.g. `HEM`, `ATP`, `STI`, `MK1`)
  - Full `.pdb` Cartesian atomic coordinate blocks
- **How BioInsight AI Uses the Data**:
  - Displays experimental structures in the 3D Structure Explorer (`/structures`).
  - Fetches target receptor proteins for the AutoDock Vina Molecular Docking Platform (`/docking`).
  - Isolates co-crystallized ligands to calculate binding pocket centroids and grid dimensions.
- **Attribution & Citation Requirement**:
  - Mandatory citation of the RCSB PDB core resource and primary structure literature:
    > *Berman HM, et al. The Protein Data Bank. Nucleic Acids Res. 2000 Jan 1;28(1):235-42. doi: 10.1093/nar/28.1.235.*
    > *Burley SK, et al. RCSB Protein Data Bank: powerful new tools for exploring 3D structures of biological macromolecules for basic and applied research and education in fundamental biology, biomedicine, biotechnology, healthcare and energy. Nucleic Acids Res. 2021 Jan 8;49(D1):D437-D451.*
- **License & Terms of Use**:
  - **CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)**. Data in the PDB archive is freely available without copyright restrictions.
  - RCSB PDB Usage Policies: [https://www.rcsb.org/pages/usage-policy](https://www.rcsb.org/pages/usage-policy)
- **Rate Limits & API Policies**:
  - Zero-hit responses return HTTP 204 (No Content), which BioInsight AI safely parses as `0 hits` without throwing errors.

### 4. PubChem (NCBI / NLM)

- **Official Website**: [https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/)
- **API Documentation**: [https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/pug-rest](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/pug-rest) (PUG REST)
- **Primary Endpoint Used**: `https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/rest/pug/compound/name/{name}/JSON` and `/property/{props}/JSON`
- **Data Retrieved**:
  - Compound Identifiers (CID), IUPAC chemical names, and synonyms
  - Canonical SMILES and InChIKey representations
  - Computed physicochemical properties: Molecular weight ($g/mol$), XLogP, topological polar surface area (TPSA), rotatable bonds, hydrogen bond donor/acceptor counts
  - 3D conformer coordinates in `.sdf` format
- **How BioInsight AI Uses the Data**:
  - Drives small-molecule search in Drug & Compound Intelligence (`/compounds`).
  - Evaluates Lipinski's Rule of 5 for oral druglikeness.
  - Provides canonical SMILES inputs for ligand conformer preparation in AutoDock Vina.
- **Attribution & Citation Requirement**:
  - Cite the PubChem resource paper:
    > *Kim S, et al. PubChem 2023 update. Nucleic Acids Res. 2023 Jan 6;51(D1):D1373-D1380. doi: 10.1093/nar/gkac956.*
- **License & Terms of Use**:
  - **Public Domain**: Data compiled by PubChem is generally in the public domain (US Government work).
  - PubChem Data Usage Policy: [https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/open-access](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/open-access)
- **Rate Limits & API Policies**:
  - PUG REST rate limits: No more than 5 requests per second, 400 requests per minute, and 300 seconds of execution time per minute. Requests are throttled and cached locally in PostgreSQL (`compound_cache`).

### 7. AlphaFold Protein Structure Database (DeepMind / EMBL-EBI)

- **Official Website**: [https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/](https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/)
- **API Documentation**: [https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/api-docs](https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/api-docs)
- **Primary Endpoint Used**: `https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/api/prediction/{uniprot_accession}`
- **Data Retrieved**:
  - Predicted 3D coordinates for whole-proteome protein structures
  - Predicted Local Distance Difference Test (pLDDT) confidence scores:
    - Very high confidence ($> 90$)
    - Confident ($70 - 90$)
    - Low confidence ($50 - 70$)
    - Very low confidence ($< 50$)
  - Predicted Aligned Error (PAE) and CIF/PDB coordinate downloads
- **How BioInsight AI Uses the Data**:
  - Surfaces computational structure predictions in the 3D Structure Explorer (`/structures`).
  - Separates computational predictions from experimental PDB structures to avoid false scientific equivalence.
  - Implements an automated UniProt fallback query when users search AlphaFold DB using gene symbols (e.g., `BRCA1`, `TP53`, `EGFR`).
- **Attribution & Citation Requirement**:
  - Mandatory citation under CC BY 4.0:
    > *Jumper J, et al. Highly accurate protein structure prediction with AlphaFold. Nature. 2021 Aug;596(7873):583-589. doi: 10.1038/s41586-021-03819-2.*

- **License & Terms of Use**:
  - **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**.

  - AlphaFold DB Terms: [https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/about](https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/about)

## 3. Scientific Responsibility, Reproducibility & Disclaimers

### Data Provenance Categorization

To maintain complete scientific integrity, BioInsight AI 2.0 explicitly classifies every displayed output into one of three categories:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        DATA PROVENANCE TIERS                           │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Direct Authoritative Data                                           │
│    • Unmodified records retrieved live from official scientific APIs.  │
│    • Examples: UniProt sequences, RCSB PDB crystal structures,        │
│      NCBI genomic coordinates, PubChem SMILES.                         │
│                                                                        │
│ 2. Deterministic Computed Outputs                                      │
│    • Exact mathematical and physicochemical values computed locally.    │
│    • Examples: Biopython ProtParam (MW, pI, GRAVY, instability),       │
│      Needleman-Wunsch sequence alignments, Lipinski Rule of 5.         │
│                                                                        │
│ 3. Computational Predictions & In Silico Estimates                     │
│    • Algorithmic predictions subject to uncertainty and force-field    │
│      approximations.                                                   │
│    • Examples: AlphaFold DB pLDDT confidence scores,                   │
│      AutoDock Vina predicted binding affinities (ΔG in kcal/mol).      │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### AutoDock Vina In Silico Docking Disclaimer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **COMPUTATIONAL ESTIMATE NOTICE**:
>
> The binding affinities ($\Delta G$ in $\text{kcal/mol}$) and docked poses generated by the **AutoDock Vina v1.2.5** engine in BioInsight AI 2.0 are **computational approximations** derived from empirical scoring functions and rigid-receptor / flexible-ligand optimization algorithms.
>
> - AutoDock Vina scores **do not constitute experimental binding free energies** (e.g., as measured by Isothermal Titration Calorimetry (ITC), Surface Plasmon Resonance (SPR), or enzyme inhibition assays).
> - Computational docking results **do not establish clinical safety, pharmacological efficacy, or biological activity in living systems**.
> - All virtual screening hits and predicted poses **must undergo rigorous experimental synthesis, biochemical validation, and in vitro / in vivo verification** before any clinical, therapeutic, or diagnostic conclusions may be drawn.
